## Context

Yuzora Tea House sells tea that is often a gift. Gift buyers need guidance, a finished-looking box,
a delivery date and a note; companies buy in volume for clients and teams.

## Approach

- **Gifting as the main flow.** A three-tea box builder with an automatic discount, delivery date
  and gift note in the cart, and a branded gift card.
- **Corporate tiers without B2B.** Native volume pricing is B2B-only, so three automatic discounts
  and a quote form cover bulk orders.
- **Content that sells the product.** Brewing guides as metaobject pages attached to each tea.

## Process

Each feature starts as a spec with acceptance criteria and an estimate, ships through a pull
request with a self review, and passes Theme Check before merge. Every Admin GraphQL operation is
validated against the schema before it runs.
