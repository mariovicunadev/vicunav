## The brief

Most Yuzora orders are gifts. A gift buyer needs help choosing, a box that looks finished, a
delivery date and a note. Companies order the same gifts by the dozen for clients and teams.

## How it's built

- A box builder: pick three teas, see the box, and get the set price automatically.
- Delivery date and gift note in the cart, and a gift card designed for the brand.
- Volume pricing in Shopify is B2B-only, so three automatic discount tiers and a quote form
  handle corporate orders.
- Each tea links to its own brewing guide page.
