## The brief

Bhoga Yoga teaches small groups in San Carlos del Zulia, Venezuela. Two kinds of people walk in:
beginners who have never done yoga, and athletes who want mobility and fewer injuries. The site
has to speak to both and get them to book a first class.

## How it's built

- One Elementor Pro page with a section for each audience, schedules, the teachers, questions
  and a WhatsApp booking button.
- Rank Math sets the local business data, with address and map coordinates, and Site Kit
  connects Search Console and Analytics.
- Privacy and terms pages.
- It shares the AWS server with Tati Pilates, isolated with its own user, PHP pool and database.
