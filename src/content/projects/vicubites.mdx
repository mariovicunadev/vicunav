## The brief

Vicubites sells premium dog and cat food to pet owners, who buy once or on autoship, and to vet
clinics, pet shops and groomers, who buy wholesale on the same store. Every bag is defined by its
nutrition, so most of what a shopper needs is data: protein, life stage, kcal, ingredients.

## How it's built

I kept Horizon and Shopify's own apps wherever they were enough, and wrote code only where they
stopped.

- Nine typed metafields and an ingredient metaobject replace tags, so filters, product blocks and
  ingredient pages read the same data the merchant edits.
- Badge, Benefit, Ingredients and Purchase options are theme blocks that drop into any section.
- The feeding calculator, the autoship picker and the filters work before JavaScript loads.
