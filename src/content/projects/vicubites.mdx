## Context

Vicubites sells premium dog and cat food to two audiences on the same store: pet owners who buy
once or on autoship, and vet clinics, pet shops and groomers who buy wholesale. The catalog is
nutrition-heavy (proteins, life stages, ingredients), so most product information is data,
not prose.

## Approach

- **Native first.** Horizon, Search & Discovery and Shopify Subscriptions cover most needs; custom
  code goes only where they stop.
- **Typed data instead of tags.** Nine `custom` product metafields and an `Ingredient` metaobject
  drive filters, product page blocks and ingredient pages, all editable by the merchant.
- **Reusable theme blocks.** Badge, Benefit, Ingredients and Purchase options work in any
  section that accepts blocks.
- **Progressive enhancement.** Every interactive piece (calculator, autoship picker, filters) has a
  working no-JavaScript baseline.

## Process

Each feature starts as a spec with acceptance criteria and an estimate, ships through a pull
request with a self review, and passes Theme Check with no errors before merge.
