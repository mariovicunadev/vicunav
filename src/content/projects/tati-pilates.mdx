## The brief

Tati Pilates is a studio in Punto Fijo, Venezuela, teaching small groups inside a local gym. The
owner needed two things: a site that brings in new students from local searches, and one place
to manage classes, plans, payments and bookings that her students could also use.

## How it's built

The public site and the studio system are one WordPress install.

- The site is a single Elementor Pro page that goes from the problems a new student has to
  schedules, prices, the instructor's story, questions and a WhatsApp button.
- A private plugin adds the studio: schedules with spots per class, students on their own
  WordPress accounts, weekly and individual plans, bookings, makeup sessions, attendance,
  monthly payments, reminders and a student portal at `/mi-pilates`.
- Prices are entered once in the plugin and appear on the Elementor page.
- The site runs on a server I set up on AWS and moved it to from shared hosting.

## Results

The studio's schedule, students and payments live in one admin, and students book their own
classes within their plan from the portal.
