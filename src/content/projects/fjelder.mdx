## Context

Fjelder sells technical layers to shoppers who compare specs: waterproof rating, weight, fill.
The store's base currency is the Canadian dollar and it sells across three markets in three
languages, so cross-border and catalog structure matter as much as the look.

## Approach

- **One listing, many colors.** Each color is its own product for media, SEO and inventory, combined
  under one listing with swatches.
- **Specs as filters.** Typed metafields drive Search & Discovery filters by activity, waterproof
  rating, weight and color.
- **Cross-border from day one.** Markets for the US, Canada and the EU with EN, ES and FR content.
- **Demand capture without an app.** Back-in-stock requests with Shopify Forms and Flow.

## Process

Each feature starts as a spec with acceptance criteria and an estimate, ships through a pull
request with a self review, and passes Theme Check before merge. Every Admin GraphQL operation is
validated against the schema before it runs.
