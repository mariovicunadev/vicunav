## The brief

Nelson Look Flash is a photography studio in Santa Bárbara del Zulia, Venezuela, shooting studio,
editorial, outdoor, children's and social sessions. Clients choose a photographer by looking at
work like theirs, so the site is the portfolio.

## How it's built

- A WPBakery site extended through a child theme, so updates to the parent never overwrite the
  custom work.
- One page per type of session, reachable from the main menu.
- A booking page with a form that asks for the reason for the session and the location.
- Yoast SEO with organization data and breadcrumbs, and Site Kit for Search Console and
  Analytics.
