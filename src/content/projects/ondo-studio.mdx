## Context

Ondo Studio builds furniture to order. Two audiences buy on the same store: home shoppers who
choose a wood finish and a fabric, and interior designers and hospitality buyers who order at
trade prices with payment terms.

## Approach

- **B2B on the same store.** Companies, catalogs, price lists, quantity rules and net terms, with
  the theme showing trade prices and price breaks.
- **Materials as data.** Finish and fabric metaobjects with swatches feed a configurator that sends
  the choice to cart and order.
- **De-risking a large purchase.** Free fabric swatches and clear lead times on product and cart.
- **Rooms that sell.** Room pages list their pieces and add the set to the cart.

## Process

Each feature starts as a spec with acceptance criteria and an estimate, ships through a pull
request with a self review, and passes Theme Check before merge. Every Admin GraphQL operation is
validated against the schema before it runs.
