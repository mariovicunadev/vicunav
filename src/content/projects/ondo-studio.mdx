## The brief

Ondo Studio builds furniture to order. Homeowners pick a wood and a fabric; interior designers and
hotels order at trade prices with payment terms. Both buy on the same store.

## How it's built

- B2B companies, trade price lists, quantity rules and net 30 terms, with trade prices and price
  breaks shown in the theme.
- Finishes and fabrics are metaobjects with swatches; the choice travels to the cart and the
  order.
- Free fabric swatches and a lead time on every product make a big purchase less risky.
- Room pages list their pieces and add the whole room to the cart.
