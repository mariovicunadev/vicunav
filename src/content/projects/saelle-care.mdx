## Context

Saelle Care sells a short skincare routine: cleanse, tone, treat, moisturize, plus refills. Shoppers
choose by skin type and by step, and they research ingredients, so the store is driven by typed
data rather than product copy.

## Approach

- **Brand as data.** Colors, type, radius and motion live in one tokens file that writes the theme
  settings, the brand book and the social images, behind a WCAG contrast gate.
- **A store rebuilt from the repository.** Catalog, size variants, ingredient metaobjects,
  collections, pages, journal, policies and menus load with one idempotent command.
- **Product pages built from data.** Skin type, key ingredients and how-to-use come from metafields
  and metaobjects the merchant edits in the admin.
- **Native first.** Shopify Bundles, Subscriptions and automatic discounts before any custom app.

## Process

Each feature starts as a spec with acceptance criteria and an estimate, ships through a pull
request with a self review, and passes Theme Check before merge. Every Admin GraphQL operation is
validated against the schema before it runs.
