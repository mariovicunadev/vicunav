## Context

Kairon Audio sells headphones and speakers to shoppers who decide on specs: battery life, driver
size, weight, codecs. The store has to make comparing easy and sell protection and accessories
without getting in the way.

## Approach

- **Compare before buying.** Up to three products side by side, rendered with the Section
  Rendering API from spec metafields.
- **Filters that match how people think.** Range filters for battery life, weight and driver size.
- **Attach rate.** A two-year warranty add-on on the product form and complementary accessories.
- **Media that shows build quality.** Video and 3D models in the gallery where assets exist.

## Process

Each feature starts as a spec with acceptance criteria and an estimate, ships through a pull
request with a self review, and passes Theme Check before merge. Every Admin GraphQL operation is
validated against the schema before it runs.
