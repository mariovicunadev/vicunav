## The brief

Kairon Audio's customers read spec sheets. The store has to make comparing easy, then offer
protection and accessories without getting in the way.

## How it's built

- Compare up to three products side by side, from the same spec fields as the product page.
- Range filters for battery life, weight and driver size.
- A two-year warranty that goes into the cart with the product, plus accessories that pair with
  it.
- Video and 3D models in the gallery where they exist.
