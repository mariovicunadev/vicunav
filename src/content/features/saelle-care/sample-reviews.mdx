---
title: Sample reviews, done honestly
project: saelle-care
problem: A demo store needs reviews to show the design, but fake reviews mislead shoppers and search engines.
decision: Review metaobjects linked to products, a star summary and highlights, an always-visible "written with AI" note, and no review structured data.
status: shipped
surfaces: [theme, data]
skills: [Metaobjects, Structured data, Liquid, Accessibility]
quality:
  - No aggregateRating or review in JSON-LD, no microdata, data-nosnippet on the block
  - Reviews editable from the product's Reviews metafield
  - Stars fill exactly, including the fraction of the last star
order: 7
---
Reviews live in the `custom` namespace on purpose: `reviews.rating` is the contract that real review
apps write and other surfaces trust, so sample data stays out of it. A rich results test of a
product page shows only the product, with no rating.
