---
title: Accessibility checked with a screen reader
project: saelle-care
problem: Automated audits pass pages that still confuse people using a screen reader or a pointer with speech.
decision: A page-by-page review with the mouse, the keyboard and NVDA, every finding traced to its cause, and minimal changes where the cause is in the base theme.
surfaces: [theme, performance]
skills: [Accessibility, ARIA, WCAG 2.2, Lighthouse]
quality:
  - Lighthouse accessibility and SEO 100 at release, mobile and desktop
  - Every color the theme renders checked against the tokens, all text at WCAG AA
  - Each finding recorded with its cause and fix in the spec
order: 15
---
A design review first brought every color the theme renders back to the brand tokens: section
backgrounds kept from the base theme, muted text drawn with opacity and a few hard-coded values
passed the token check but not WCAG. Type, elevation and motion were refined in the same pass.

The screen reader review then found what the audits missed. Menu items without a submenu were
announced as "collapsed", the hero and the journal and collection cards read only their image under
the pointer because of a full-card link overlay, and dialogs left focus on the page body when they
closed. Each one was fixed at its cause, and the changes to theme core are listed in the spec.
