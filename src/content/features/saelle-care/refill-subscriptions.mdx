---
title: Refills on subscription
project: saelle-care
problem: Refills are the products customers buy again, so they should be able to subscribe without leaving the product page.
decision: Shopify Subscriptions owns the plans and the renewal billing; the theme renders its own purchase options block that reads those plans.
surfaces: [theme, checkout]
skills: [Selling plans, Shopify Subscriptions, Liquid, Progressive enhancement]
quality:
  - Tested end to end with a test order; the contract appears in Shopify Subscriptions
  - The block joins the product form with the HTML form attribute, without touching theme core
order: 11
---
Plans (every 4, 8 or 12 weeks, 10% off) are created in Shopify Subscriptions, not by a script: the
app that owns a plan is the one that bills renewals and lists the contracts. The theme reads them
through `product.selling_plan_groups`, swaps the price when the shopper picks "Subscribe and save"
and shows the plan under the line in the cart.

A disabled field is not submitted, so switching back to one-time purchase removes the plan from the
request with no script logic. Checkout shows the recurring subtotal and the delivery frequency.
