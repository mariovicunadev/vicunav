---
title: Key actives and the rest of the routine
project: saelle-care
problem: A product page has to say who it is for, what is in it and what to use with it, without the merchant writing that copy for every product.
decision: An identity line, key actives with their concentration from a join metaobject, a shared benefits group and complementary products from Search & Discovery, each block hidden when its data is missing.
surfaces: [theme, data]
skills: [Metaobjects, Search & Discovery, Theme blocks, Accessibility]
quality:
  - axe reports 0 violations and Lighthouse accessibility is 100 on the product page
  - Blocks render nothing when their data is missing, so no empty headings
order: 14
---
Concentrations depend on the product, not on the ingredient, so a `key_active` metaobject joins the
two; refills share their formula's entries. "How refills and subscriptions work" comes from one
benefits group used by every product, and "Complete the routine" reads Search & Discovery's
complementary products.

The base theme's sticky add-to-cart button had no accessible name. It is fixed in the store's own
code, without editing the theme file.
