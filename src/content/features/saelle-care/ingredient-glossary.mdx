---
title: An ingredient glossary built from data
project: saelle-care
problem: Shoppers look up ingredients before they buy, and a page written by hand for each one goes out of date.
decision: The ingredient metaobject publishes its own web pages, with an A–Z glossary built from the same entries and product pages linking to them.
surfaces: [theme, data]
skills: [Metaobjects, SEO, Liquid]
quality:
  - SEO title and description come from each entry's fields
  - Every ingredient page lists the products that use it
order: 12
---
Turning on the metaobject's online store capability gives each ingredient a URL and a template,
`templates/metaobject/ingredient.json`. The glossary page groups every entry by letter, and the
ingredient chips on a product page link to those pages.

Liquid can't look up which products reference a metaobject, so "Found in" scans the catalog for the
ingredient when the page renders. That holds for a catalog this size; the limit and the trade-off are
documented in the spec.
