---
title: "Starter kits: native bundle vs. Cart Transform"
project: vicubites
problem: New customers want a starter kit, and one kit has a single fixed content while the other lets them pick a recipe and a flavor.
decision: The fixed kit is a native bundle created through the Admin API; the configurable kit uses a Cart Transform Function that expands it into the chosen products and splits the price to the cent.
surfaces: [functions, checkout, app, theme]
skills: [Shopify Functions, Cart Transform, Bundles, Theme app extensions, TypeScript]
quality:
  - Worst case measured at 10.31 M of 11 M instructions (200 lines, 18 kit lines), with the limit documented
  - Inventory and fulfillment tracked per component, checked with a test order
  - Component prices always add up to the kit price
order: 18
---
Eighteen recipe and flavor combinations would mean eighteen variants on a native bundle, so the
cat kit is one product and a theme app block collects the choice as line properties. The Function
expands the line into its two components at checkout; the order shows them grouped under the kit.
An admin page builds the Function's configuration from the store's products and warns when it needs
an update.

The margin is thin by design and stated in the README: about 25 kit lines in a full cart would pass
the limit, and growing the options would mean moving the Function to Rust.
