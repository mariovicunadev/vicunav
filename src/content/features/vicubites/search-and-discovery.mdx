---
title: Filters, search and recommendations
project: vicubites
problem: Shoppers need to narrow by pet, life stage, protein and size, and find products with everyday words like "kibble".
decision: Shopify Search & Discovery on top of the typed metafields, using the theme's existing Section Rendering filters; keyword tags replace the retired synonyms.
surfaces: [theme, data]
skills: [Search & Discovery, Section Rendering API, Metafields, Product recommendations]
quality:
  - Filters update without a full reload and the URL is shareable
  - Dry recipes show complementary products
  - Predictive search returns products for "salmon" and "puppy"
order: 8
---
The native app was chosen over a custom filter: Horizon already renders storefront filters with the
Section Rendering API and ships predictive search, so the work was configuration on top of the data
model. Complementary products ("Pairs well with") are written by script into the metafield the app
reads.

When search synonyms were retired, keyword tags took their place, so a search for "kibble" or
"pup" still finds the right products.
