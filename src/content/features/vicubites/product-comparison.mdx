---
title: Product comparison section
project: vicubites
problem: Shoppers choosing between similar recipes had to open each product and compare protein, life stage and price per kg in their head.
decision: A section where the merchant picks 2–4 products; a semantic table compares attributes, sizes and price per kg, reading attributes through one snippet.
status: shipped
surfaces: [theme]
skills: [Liquid, Semantic HTML, Accessibility, Metafields]
quality:
  - Semantic table with th scope, readable at 360 px
  - Fewer than 2 products shows an editor-only notice
  - Theme Check passes with no new errors
order: 6
---
Price per kg is computed in Liquid from each variant's price and weight, so it updates when a price
changes. Attributes are read through a single snippet documented with LiquidDoc: when the catalog
moved from tags to typed metafields, only that snippet changed and the section kept working.
