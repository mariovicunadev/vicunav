title: Checkout rules with Shopify Functions
project: vicubites
problem: Customers who reorder on time should get a reward, and retail buyers shouldn't place wholesale-sized orders, without a script running outside Shopify's checkout.
decision: Two Functions in TypeScript, a product discount and a cart validation, configured from an app page that edits each instance without a redeploy.
surfaces: [functions, checkout, app]
skills: [Shopify Functions, Discounts, Cart validation, TypeScript, Performance]
quality:
  - "Measured on 200-line carts: 7.45 M and 6.73 M instructions of the 11 M limit"
  - Unit tests with typed input fixtures, plus a contract test between the admin page and each Function
  - "Both fail open: missing settings mean no discount and no error, and a failing validation never blocks checkout"
order: 17
---
The refill reward discounts dry food when the customer's predicted refill date is within 14 days,
before or after. The order limit caps six units per variant for retail buyers and points them to a
B2B account; B2B buyers are not limited.

Testing in the store changed two decisions. A validation error during cart interaction makes the
Ajax Cart API reject every change, removals included, so the limit runs only at checkout steps. And
a subscription line could take two discounts, so it is excluded.

Half of a Function's cost is parsing its input. A first version of the order limit built with
`flatMap` and a `Map` cost 9.1 M at 200 lines; plain loops brought it to 6.73 M.
