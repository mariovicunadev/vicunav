---
title: Autoship with selling plans
project: vicubites
problem: Repeat customers want food to arrive on a schedule without leaving the product page or creating a separate subscription.
decision: Shopify Subscriptions for billing and a custom selling plan picker in the theme, bound to the product form with the HTML form attribute so it works without JavaScript.
status: shipped
surfaces: [theme, app]
skills: [Selling plans, Shopify Subscriptions, Liquid, Progressive enhancement, B2B]
quality:
  - Prices read from selling_plan_allocations, never recalculated in the theme
  - Selected plan survives a variant (bag size) change
  - Spec, PR with self review, Theme Check with 0 errors
order: 2
---

Shopify Subscriptions owns the contracts and billing; the theme owns the experience. The picker
lists each delivery frequency with its per-delivery price taken from the variant's
`selling_plan_allocations`, so discounts configured in the app are always what the shopper sees.

Inputs sit outside the product form in the markup but submit with it through the `form`
attribute. No script is needed to send `selling_plan` with the cart request, and the same
contract is what any subscription app (Recharge, Skio, Loop) reads.

B2B buyers on the blended store get the standard buy box: Shopify doesn't allow subscription
purchases in a B2B context, so the picker renders only when `customer.b2b?` is false and the
product has selling plans.
