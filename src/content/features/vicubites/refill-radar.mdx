title: "Refill Radar: reminders before the bag runs out"
project: vicubites
problem: Pet food is bought again on a rhythm, and a customer who runs out before reordering may buy elsewhere.
decision: The app predicts each customer's run-out date from their orders; the storefront, Klaviyo and Flow deliver the reminder.
surfaces: [app, theme, data]
skills: [Webhooks, App metafields, Theme app extensions, Web pixels, Klaviyo, Shopify Flow]
quality:
  - Reorders counted server-side from orders, so ad blockers and consent don't hide them
  - Reminder events carry a unique id, so a retried event is not counted twice
  - Customer data limited to what a reminder needs, read when sending and not stored
order: 16
---
Every new order arrives through a webhook. The app estimates when the food runs out (bag size and
daily portion, or the customer's own reorder rhythm once there is one) and stores the date in the
app's own metafields.

A theme app block shows "Time to restock?" on the home page with a one-click reorder of the exact
bag, closest pet first. A `Refill due` event goes to Klaviyo for the email, and a Flow trigger lets
the merchant build their own automation without code. B2B companies sync to HubSpot with their
contacts.

The web pixel measures views and clicks as a minimum; the reorders themselves are counted from
orders on the server.
