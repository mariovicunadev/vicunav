---
title: Star ratings that work with any review app
project: vicubites
problem: Product cards showed no ratings, and wiring the theme to one review app would lock the merchant in.
decision: Read Shopify's standard reviews.rating and reviews.rating_count metafields, which review apps write, so any app works without theme changes.
surfaces: [theme, data]
skills: [Metafields, Liquid, Accessibility, Structured data]
quality:
  - Cards show stars and count only for rated products
  - Rating exposed to screen readers as text; stars are decorative
  - No aggregateRating emitted while ratings are sample data
order: 7
---
The standard definitions were enabled with `standardMetafieldDefinitionEnable` and filled with
sample values by an idempotent script. Because Judge.me, Okendo and Yotpo write to the same
contract, switching apps needs no theme work.

The sample ratings are not real reviews, so the theme deliberately emits no `aggregateRating`
structured data while they are in place.
